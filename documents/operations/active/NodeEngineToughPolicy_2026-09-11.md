# --node Engine: Tough Policy (owner decree, 2026-09-11)

Owner, verbatim intent: *"I want a tough policy on the --node engine. No easy passes, a rigorous test of rustynet in any way imaginable. Any way you think rustynet could be exploited needs to be defended against; we defend against it with these live labs."*

This is a standing rule for everyone working on `crates/rustynet-cli/src/vm_lab/**` and the lab bins. It ranks with `Requirements.md`/`SecurityMinimumBar.md` for the lab: when a stage's strictness and its convenience conflict, strictness wins.

## Rules

1. **A pass is a measured claim, never a default.** Every `Passed` carries a declared witness with a per-run datum (QH-83); a stage that cannot measure reports `Skipped(reason)` or `Failed`, never `Passed`. Vacuous witnesses (constant lines, `nodes=0` passes) are defects.
2. **The lab distrusts the guest.** A validator that lets the guest's own report decide the verdict (`overall_ok=true` without row-by-row agreement, a status line the daemon writes about itself) must cross-check with an independent observation (packet capture, nft dump, a second node's view, a probe the daemon cannot influence).
3. **Every documented attack gets a live stage.** The threat catalog (`SecurityMinimumBar.md`, `tools/skills/rustynet-security-auditor`, the RSA findings in `AdversarialSecurityRemediation*`) maps to stages: a control with no live-lab exercise is an open item on `QualityHardeningTodo`, not a footnote. Negative controls (sabotage applied AND detected) are the proof shape for detection claims; chaos stages for resilience claims.
4. **Environment gaps are named, never absorbed.** A missing tool, module, package or topology role is a `Skipped`/`Failed` naming the precondition (the `requires()` class), so a green run can never mean "the dangerous half didn't execute".
5. **Reviews are adversarial and grounded.** New stages and changed criteria get a GLM review briefed to break them (file:line), and a live re-verify on both hosts before an evidence claim.
6. **No bypasses.** The launch gate, the reuse seal, the witness gate and the provenance check have no force flags; a decline is attributed to a commit and re-blocks when the tree moves.

## Standing work generated by this policy

- Attack-coverage gap audit (threat catalog × stage catalog) → proposed negative-control/chaos/adversarial stages, each with a fail-closed criterion — review H, `state/review/r2/H.out`.
- Pass-criteria strength audit ("could a broken product pass this stage?") across all 76 rows — review I, `state/review/r2/I.out`.
- Both feed `QualityHardeningTodo_2026-07-25.md` as numbered QH items with owners.
